Privacy Policy

Last updated · July 2026

MATIN SHAPE keeps this simple: we collect as little as possible, we never sell your information, and we treat anything you share with us the way we'd want our own business details treated.

What we collect

  • Contact details you send us — name, email, company, and anything you write in the contact form or emails, used only to respond and work together.
  • Basic technical data — this site stores small amounts of data in your own browser (such as anonymous page-performance measurements). This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to us or to third parties.

What we don't do

  • No selling or renting of personal information — ever.
  • No third-party advertising trackers or marketing cookies.
  • No sharing of client business information without written permission.

Client project confidentiality

Anything we learn about your business during an engagement — processes, numbers, tools, problems — is treated as confidential by default. Client work is only referenced publicly with explicit approval.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email hello@matinshape.studio and we'll handle it promptly.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated. Material changes will be noted clearly.
