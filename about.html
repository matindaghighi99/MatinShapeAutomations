The studio

Small studio. Obsessive standards.

MATIN SHAPE was founded on a simple observation: the most valuable people in every company spend half their week on work a machine should do. We exist to give that time back — calmly.

"Luxury isn't gold leaf and marble. Luxury is a Tuesday afternoon where nothing is on fire — because the systems simply work."

— The MATIN SHAPE founding principle

The founder

Designed by Matin. Personally. Every time.

MATIN DAGHIGHI Founder & Systems Designer
Founder & Systems Designer Matin Daghighi

If a task repeats itself, someone forgot to design it away.

Matin started MATIN SHAPE after watching the same story repeat inside good companies: talented people copying data between tools, chasing follow-ups from memory, answering the same question forty times a week. Not because anyone chose that — because nobody had designed the alternative.

So the studio designs the alternative. Not "automation" as a pile of Zapier triggers, but as a considered system: mapped against how your business actually operates, built with the fewest moving parts that will do the job, documented like a product, and boring in the best possible sense of the word.

Matin designs every blueprint himself and brings in trusted specialists per build — voice, data, integrations — so every system ships with senior hands on it and one person accountable for the whole: him.

  • 01Watch first, build second. Every engagement starts with a week inside your real workflows.
  • 02Say no early. If an automation won't pay for itself, you'll hear it on the first call.
  • 03Leave things learnable. Handover docs so clear your next hire masters them in an afternoon.

What we believe

Three rules we never break.

RULE / 01

Boring is beautiful

The best automation is the one you forget exists. We optimize for systems that run for years without drama — not demos that impress for a week.

RULE / 02

Value before code

Every workflow we propose must earn its keep — hours back, leads answered, invoices paid. If we can't articulate the return, we don't build it.

RULE / 03

Few clients, deep work

We take four new clients a quarter. Not because it sounds exclusive — because bespoke systems require the kind of attention you can't split forty ways.

The compass

What we believe — and what we refuse to build.

What MATIN SHAPE believes

  • Automation should simplify, not complicate.
  • AI should help people work better — not replace the human side of a business.
  • Beautiful systems are easier to trust, monitor, and maintain.
  • Strategy matters more than tools. Always.
  • Every workflow should have a clear owner, a fallback, and a purpose.
  • The best automation feels quiet when it works and obvious when it needs attention.

What we never build

  • Messy automations with no strategy behind them.
  • Fake AI demos that don't survive contact with real operations.
  • Systems with no human fallback when something unusual happens.
  • Tool stacks the client can't understand or maintain.
  • Overcomplicated workflows built just to look advanced.
